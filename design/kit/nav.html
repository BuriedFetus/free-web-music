  1. Double-click a pattern (Groove, Beat…): the Piano roll comes up full size with it. Esc or ← goes back.
  2. Double-click an instrument (BASALT…): its Device, full size.
  3. Or the small ↗ on the hovered / selected one (one tap: for touch screens and the hardware).
  4. Hover the top bar icons: tips after a moment, then at once while you slide along.
  5. Make people join and leave: the bar doesn't move.
