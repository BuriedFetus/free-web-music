What this proposes

  • One strip per instrument, in the order of the Song arrangement: level fader with a meter, Pan, Mute, Solo, and two sends: how much of the instrument goes to the shared Reverb and Delay. The master strip sums everything.
  • ⤢ expands the Mixer and shows the effects rack: Reverb and Delay (shared, fed by the sends), then Lo-fi and the main Low-pass on the whole mix. Signal path: instruments + Reverb + Delay → Lo-fi → Low-pass → Master.
  • The Delay follows the tempo (1/16 to dotted 1/4). Ping-pong bounces left and right.
  • Lo-fi is tape and vinyl, not a bit-crusher: Wow and Flutter (slow and fast pitch wobble), Saturation (soft tape warmth), Age (the highs and lowest lows fade), Noise (tape hiss, or darker record surface) and Crackle. Tape and Vinyl set good starting points.
  • Everything is shared like the instruments: faders, sends and effect settings are in the song; others see your hand on a fader, and undo is per person.
  • The Scope is its own panel (pick it in any of the four panel slots): the master or one instrument, a steady trigger so waves stand still, time zoom, Freeze, and a Spectrum view.
  • The sounds here are rough browser previews; the real ones run in the studio's audio engine like Basalt and Flint.
