Main sequencer · song arrangement

Each cell picks the pattern an instrument plays in that TRACK. A TRACK lasts as long as its longest pattern; shorter ones repeat inside it.

  • Mock-up, no sound. Click a cell to pick its pattern below; press Play to watch TRACKs follow each other. Drums and Arp stand in for Flint and Cobalt (Phase 5).
  • Patterns belong to an instrument. Scenes A–D are gone: Bass Pattern 2 has nothing to do with Drums Pattern 2.
  • Each pattern has its own length, from 1 step to 8 bars, and opens in the Piano roll. The song key is set per instrument too.
  • Songs: the Song menu saves, opens and starts a new song from INIT (one empty Basalt, 120 BPM). Saving is done by the host app.
