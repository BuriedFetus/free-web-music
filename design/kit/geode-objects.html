Geode: a rug, a door and a mirror

Three new things in Mica's room. Each one is one idea that moves a few knobs. Press Sound, then drag them in the room (or use the cards).

Drag the rug's corner · pull the door left to open it · drag the mirror right to turn it, click it to flip the tape

Rug

Soaks up the room. A bigger rug: the reverb tail gets darker and shorter, the slap-backs softer, and every echo repeat a little duller.

Door

Pull it left and it swings open into the room: the way out to the rest of the song. The more it is open, the more Geode goes to the song's shared Reverb and Delay (its SEND knobs).

Mirror

Drag it right to turn it toward the room: a wider stereo picture. Click it to flip the tape: Mica's reflection turns her back, and the tape plays backwards.

The room

To hear the rug and the door against something: room size and echo.
