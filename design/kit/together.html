The network between you

What this proposes

  • One Play for everyone. Whoever presses Play or Stop does it for the whole session. Everyone starts on the same beat: the studio waits a split second (a little more than the network delay) so the others can get the message in time.
  • Clicking a TRACK moves everyone when the playing TRACK ends (as Wait does now). The switch carries the exact moment, so a late message still lands on the right beat.
  • Clocks agree on their own. When someone joins, the studio sends a few quick pings to work out the difference between your clocks (Mira's computer clock is 1.2 s off here). Nobody has to do anything.
  • Joining while it plays (try "Mira reloads"): the newcomer asks for the transport and drops in at the right bar and beat.
  • Live keys: Even or Tight (per person). Even holds the other person's keys back by a small amount that follows the network, so their rhythm stays steady but slightly late. Tight plays them as soon as they arrive: sooner, but wobbly. Turn the Wobble up, Sound on, and switch between them to hear it.
  • Recording is always exact. When Mira records, her notes go into the pattern at the moment she played them on her side, so on replay they are on the beat for everyone. Only live listening has a delay.
  • Play alone (new, per person): your Play/Stop and TRACK clicks only affect you, for trying an idea without stopping the others. Edits still reach everyone. Turn it off to drop back into the group's song.
  • People chips show how far each person is (green under 150 ms, red above) and whether they're playing alone or joining.
